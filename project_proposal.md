
# Project Proposal

### Description

This will be a web application that allows users to trade items on ebay rather than just pay for them. Users can list items they are willing to give and want to get. Then they can initiate a trade if they find someone who is offering the thing they want.


### Stack Focus

Is the front-end UI or the back-end going to be the focus of your project? Or are you going to make an evenly focused full-stack application?

It will be a full-stack application using Python with Flask for backend and HTML/CSS/JavaScript (simple) as the frontend.


### Type

Will this be a website? A mobile app? Something else?

This will be a website.


### Goal

What goal will your project be designed to achieve?

Is to create an application interface that allows users to barter with their listed items. Users can go around the payment restrictions of ebay and can trade their valuables.


### Users

What kind of users will visit your app? In other words, what is the demographic of your users?
The demographic would be people who are interested in trading their unwanted things for more valuable things to them.


### Data

What data do you plan on using? How are you planning on collecting your data? You may have not picked your actual API yet, which is fine, just outline what kind of data you would like it to contain. You are welcome to create your own API and populate it with data. 

In this app I will be using the ebay API to get listings of items. I will get the data like name, pictures, and listing date. Users will be able to see listings and initiate a trade for the item using one or more of their items.
